IMC Prosperity is wrapped for Aggies, and we finished #193 overall with 487,287 XIRECs, including #56 in algorithmic trading, #2838 in manual trading, and #54 in the country rankings.

This competition was full of ups and downs across multiple rounds, including a few ideas and rounds that went negative before we found something more robust. That ended up being the best part of the experience: it forced us to think less like discretionary guessers and more like systematic traders.

Some of the strategies we worked through:

- fair-value market making with EMA and order-book imbalance signals
- drift/intercept modeling for trend products
- exact manual auction and allocation optimizers
- Black-Scholes-style voucher pricing, volatility-smile residuals, and adjacent-strike spreads
- fee-aware news/catalyst sizing for manual trades
- Round 5 product-by-product signal isolation, passive making, jump-reversion takers, and rejecting ML overlays when the validation did not hold up

The biggest lesson was that non-discretionary trading is a discipline of rules, risk limits, and honest validation. A backtest is only useful when it helps you decide what *not* to trade too.

Huge thanks to the IMC team for putting together such a thoughtful and challenging competition. Shoutout to Rian Prabhudesai, Ritej Talluri, Shrey Jha, and Gaurav Yadav for the work, ideas, and late-round resilience.

Looking forward to Prosperity 5.
